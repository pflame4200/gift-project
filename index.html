For the girl

who lives in the

positivity bubble

There's something I want you to know.

Lunch used to be

the best part of my day.

Not because of the food…

but because of you.

Sitting there, talking to you,

the view with those trees…

it just felt

peaceful.

Those conversations we had —

they never felt forced, never awkward.

It was like we had known each other

for a long time,

even though it had only been twenty days.

And honestly…

those twenty days felt like much more.

There's also something I need to say.

That day…

I came to office already upset

because of something at home.

I didn't greet you.

I didn't talk.

I didn't eat.

But you still came to me.

You pulled me to our place,

sat with me,

and kept trying to make me smile.

Now when I think about it…

I feel like I wasted that entire afternoon.

I apologize for how I behaved that day.

It won't happen again.

You introduced me to a lot of things.

To Vampire Diaries

(and yes… I will never ask for spoilers again,

you did warn me 😅)

To writing positive notes.

But more than that —

a different way of thinking.

Calmer. More positive.

And then… the pen.

You gave me something that was so personal to you.

Something you didn't even let your dad touch 😂

I didn't expect that at all.

But it meant a lot to me.

I didn't really have something like that to give you back.

So I'm giving you something

that is a part of me.

It's not just a file.

It's something that represents me.

I know you.

I know you don't like using someone else's work.

You want to build things on your own.

You want to achieve something by yourself.

And I respect that.

But this… isn't just someone else's code.

Think of it like this —

I'm just holding your hand

and helping you a little.

You wouldn't say no to that, right?

And honestly…

if you don't use it,

it might break my heart.

Because just like how you gave me your pen —

something personal,

something you never let anyone use —

I'm giving this only to you.

Not to anyone else.

Even now,

I still catch myself thinking

about that time we used to sit and talk.

You became a really good friend

in a very short time.

And I'm really glad you did.

Keep this with you.

This one's just yours 🤍
